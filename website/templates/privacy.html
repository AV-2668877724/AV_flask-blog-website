Privacy & Security Policy

At AV Postory, your privacy isn't an afterthought—it's a core feature. We believe in total transparency regarding how we handle your data and the enterprise-grade measures we use to protect it.

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a seamless publishing and social experience, we only collect the data absolutely necessary to run the platform.

  • Account Data: Your username, email address, and hashed password.
  • Profile Data: Information you voluntarily provide, such as your bio, avatar, cover photo, and social links.
  • Content Data: The posts you write, images you upload, comments you make, and messages you send in real-time chat.
  • Activity Logs: Timestamps of your logins, post creations, and basic platform interactions to ensure security and prevent abuse.

2. How We Protect Your Data

We employ modern, ironclad security protocols to ensure your data remains completely safe from unauthorized access.

  • Password Encryption: Your passwords are never stored in plain text. They are heavily encrypted using secure Werkzeug hashing algorithms.
  • XSS & CSRF Protection: Every form on our platform uses CSRF tokens, and user inputs are rigorously sanitized using Bleach to prevent malicious code injections.
  • Secure Media Storage: All images you upload are safely stored and delivered via Cloudinary's secure, encrypted Content Delivery Network (CDN).
  • Rate Limiting: We use intelligent rate-limiting to prevent spam, bot attacks, and brute-force login attempts.

3. What We Do NOT Do

AV Postory was built to be a distraction-free space. Your trust is our highest priority.

  • We do NOT sell your data: We will never sell your personal information, email, or content to advertisers or third-party data brokers.
  • No third-party trackers: We do not inject invasive advertising trackers or cookies into your browser.
  • We do NOT read your private chats: Your direct messages are private between you and the recipient. You also have the power to clear your chat history at any time.

4. Your Control & Rights

You have complete autonomy over your digital footprint on AV Postory.

  • Account Deactivation: You can temporarily deactivate your account at any time, which hides your profile and content from the public.
  • Permanent Deletion: You have the right to be forgotten. You can request a permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Data Management: You can edit or delete your posts, comments, and profile information whenever you choose.

Have Questions About Your Privacy?

We are always here to help clarify our practices or address any security concerns you might have.
